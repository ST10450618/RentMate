# RentMate Privacy Policy

RentMate is a student prototype built for PROG7314 / OPSC7312 at The IIE. This
policy exists to satisfy Google's Sign-In requirements and describes, plainly,
what the app does with your data.

## What we collect

- **From Google Sign-In:** your email address and display name.
- **From using the app:** the household, bill, chore, shopping list, and
  maintenance data you or your housemates enter.

## Where it's stored

All data is stored in a Supabase project (Postgres database) used for this
coursework project. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any
purpose beyond running the app's features (bill splitting, chore rotation,
shopping list, maintenance requests).

## Who can see it

Row-level security restricts every household's data to that household's own
members. Nobody outside your household can read your bills, chores, or
maintenance requests through the app.
