# RentMate Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 5 November 2026_

RentMate is a household management app built by students for PROG7314 at Emeris. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, where it is kept, and how to have it removed. We follow the principles of South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

## What we collect

| Data | Where it comes from | Why we need it |
|---|---|---|
| Your name and email address | Google Sign-In | To create your account and show your name to housemates |
| Household, bill, chore, shopping list and maintenance details | What you and your housemates enter | To run the app's features |
| Photos you attach to bills or maintenance requests | Your camera or gallery, only when you choose to attach one | To show housemates and your landlord the receipt or fault |
| A push notification token | Firebase Cloud Messaging, if you allow notifications | To send you alerts about bills, chores and repairs |
| Your settings (language, notification choices, biometric unlock on or off) | The Settings screen | To keep your preferences across devices |

We do **not** collect your location, contacts, or fingerprint or face data. Biometric unlock is handled entirely by Android on your device. RentMate only receives a yes/no result and never sees or stores your biometric data.

## Where it is stored

- **Supabase** (hosted in the EU): your account, household data, settings and photos.
- **Google Firebase**: notification tokens and the household activity feed.
- **On your device**: a cached copy of your household's data, so the app works offline. It is removed when you sign out or uninstall the app.

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

## Who can see it

Your household's data is visible only to members of that household. Access rules on the server enforce this, not just the app. If you email maintenance requests to your landlord, the landlord receives the request details and photo links you chose to send.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or share your data with anyone for marketing.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. If you leave a household, that household's data stays with the remaining members. Bills you were part of remain in their history, shown under your display name.

## Your rights and deleting your data

You may ask to see, correct or delete your personal information at any time. Email **royalalite@gmail.com** from the address you signed in with. We will delete your account, profile, photos and notification tokens within 30 days.

## Children

RentMate is not intended for anyone under 18.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and note the change in the app's release notes.
